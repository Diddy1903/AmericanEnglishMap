Front Vowels

/æ/ as in "trap"*, /ɛ/ as in "dress", /ɪ/ as in "kit", /ei/ as in "face"*, /ii/ as in "fleece"
*In pane-pain unmerged accents /ei/ is two phonemes, /ei/ and /e:/, derived from Middle English /ai/ and /a:/
**The BATH lexical set (historical /af/ /as/ /ath/ and /anC/) is arguably in a seperate phoneme in some dialects

/æ/,/ɛ/ retraction: Dotted-Low Back Merger (Stage 1 of "Low Back Merger Shift") Dashed- /æ/ retraction: Solid - /ɛ/ and /ɪ/ retraction.   
Northern Cities Vowel Shift: Dashed- /æ/ raising (Stage 1) Thick -  /ɛ/ and /ʌ/ retraction (Stage 3).
Southern/African American vowel shift:  thin- monophthong /ai/ (SVS Stage 1)  dotted- raised /æ/ (AAVS stage 1) Dark - /ɛ/-/ei/ reversal  (SVS/AAVS Stage 2) Darker - /ɪ/-/ii/ reversal (SVS/AAVS Stage 3)
