Consonants(voiced in bold):
Stops*: /p/ "pit", /t/ "tin", /k/ "cut",
/b/ "bit", /d/ "din", /g/ "gut"

Fricatives*: /s/ "sap", /f/ "fat", /θ/ "thigh", /ʃ/ "shoe"
/z/ "zap", /v/ "vat", /ð/ "this", /ʒ/ "pleasure"

Affricates: /tʃ/ "cheep", /dʒ/ "jeep"
*Voiceless stops are typically aspirated. Voiced stops can be realised as implosives in Jamaican and some Surinamese creoles. Implosive /b/ can also occur in the USA.
**/ʃ,ʒ,tʃ,dʒ/ merged with /s,ts/ for many Native Americans. mergers of /ʒ/=/
