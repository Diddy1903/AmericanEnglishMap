Oceania

Current and former US territories in Oceania. This includes:
The State of Hawaii
Former Commonwealth of Philippines
Former Trust Territories of the Pacific Islands
Territories of American Samoa, The Northern Marianas and Guam

Accents in this region tend to have mergers of /i/-/ii/("hid"="heed"),
monophthongal /ei/ and /ou/, backed /uu/ and /au/, some t-flapping,
and merging of /p,t,k/ and /b,d,g/.
