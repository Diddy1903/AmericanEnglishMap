Low Back Vowels

tense: /a:/ as in "father", /ɒ/ as in "lot"**, /ɔ:/ as in "thought"
*The /a:/ phoneme, distinct from /æ/ is absent prior to the late 18th century
**The CLOTH set (historical /ɒf/,/ɒs/ and /ɒθ/ words) is shifted to /C:/ in North America and variably in the Caribbean.
