Central Vowels

tense: /^/ as in "strut", /@/ as in "comma", /u/ as in "foot",New England short-oProsody: syllable-timing vs stress timing

The New England Short O (the identical British English phenomenon is called the East Anglian Short-O) is an now extinct phenomenon where /ou/ words originating in the Middle English /):/ sound (such as whole, stone) were sometimes shifted to a new vowel [8], rather than merging the usual /ou/ vowel.
