Dialects without a BATH split or any chain shifts, can show a few other shifts. /a/ can show minimal variation, particularly in Latino Englishes. The Western system, where /ă/ is tenser before nasals than elsewhere, and raising before /g/. /e/ and /i/ can also merge before /n/ in the south.
