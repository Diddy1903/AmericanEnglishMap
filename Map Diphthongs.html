Diphthongs

/uu/ as in "goose", /ou/ as in "goat"*, /au/ as in "mouth"
/ei/ as in "face", /ii/ as in "fleece", /ai/ as in "price", /oi/ as in "choice"
*For accents with New England Short-O, /ou/ is two phonemes, /ow/ and /ɵ/, derived from Middle English /ow/ and /o:/

Smoothing of /ai/:
Dashed = glide-weakening of /ai/
solid = monophthongal /ai/ + voiced obstruents
dark red= predominantly monophthongal /ai/

Central /ai/ and /au/:
Small dash=Canadian raising of /ai/
big-dash=Canadian raising of /ai/&/au/
thin line=Older Tidewater Raising of /ai/&/au/
thick=/ai/&/au/ always central
